Pokémon 6v6 Battle Arena — Champion Mechanics Update

Added/fixed:
1. Gigantamax now lasts exactly 3 turns, then automatically returns to normal form.
2. Mega Evolution remains active while the Pokémon is alive; when it faints its display state resets to original form.
3. Leech Seed is cleared when a seeded Pokémon faints, preventing the old stuck/dead target bug.
4. When an opponent Pokémon faints, the game asks whether you want to switch before the opponent's next Pokémon enters.
5. Champion AI now prioritizes damaging moves based on type effectiveness and expected power.
6. Sand Stream creates 5-turn sandstorm; Sandstorm and Hail/Snow weather damage non-immune Pokémon at end of turn.
7. Rain Dance, Sandstorm and Hail/Snow display weather visuals and 5-turn duration.
8. Existing UI, sprites, music, team selection, Mega/Bond systems and battle flow preserved.
